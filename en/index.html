Our Philosophy

A holistic approach to dentistry

At Airway Clinic, we unite scientific excellence with a holistic understanding of how teeth, jaws, breathing and sleep are interconnected. We treat the cause — not just the symptoms.

For over 30 years, Dr. Sia has helped patients of all ages achieve better breathing, sleep and quality of life through integrated airway dentistry. Our clinic in Stockholm offers a new kind of dental care — one that connects the health of teeth and jaws with breathing, posture, sleep and overall wellbeing.

We combine the art of excellent dental care with an understanding of healthy function, natural growth and balance to create lasting improvements in health and appearance.

Our Services

Specialised care for the whole family

From the youngest children to adults — we offer an integrated approach to airway health, jaw development and functional dentistry.

🌱

Growth Guidance

Gentle guidance of children's jaw growth for optimal facial development and healthy airways. Early intervention delivers lasting results.

💪

Myofunctional Therapy

Training of tongue position, swallowing and breathing patterns. Jessica Gorlee, our certified OMFT therapist, tailors each programme to your needs.

😴

Airway & Sleep Analysis

Identifying and treating breathing disorders that affect sleep, focus and quality of life. We analyse patterns and create a personalised treatment plan.

⚙️

ALF Therapy

Advanced appliances (Advanced Light Force) supporting craniofacial balance, neurological function and natural bodily harmony.

🦷

Jaw Functional Orthopaedics

JFO (Jaw Functional Orthopaedics) — gentle guidance of children's jaws in the right direction without surgery, for optimal function and aesthetics.

🔄

TMS / TMJ Treatment

Relief from jaw joint pain, tension and dysfunction. We address the root cause of your discomfort with functional therapy.

✨

General Dentistry

Professional dental care with a focus on functional health and aesthetics. We strive for excellence in every interaction with our patients.
